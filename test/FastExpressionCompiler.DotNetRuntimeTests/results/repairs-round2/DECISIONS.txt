Further repair decisions and limits

The direct emitter and object[] closure layout are preserved. Ordinary Light
nodes are trusted as the user requested; explicit intrinsic callbacks retain
their collection scopes. The rejected generic-provenance check added cold bytes.
No new excluded tests, System fallback, hosting/security-policy changes, or
deep-tree refactor is introduced. The two known deep-tree crash modes remain
deferred under the existing exclusions.

Keep this PR a draft: universal pre-PR performance parity is NOT established.
Every measured construction/compilation/first-use/runtime/activation phase is
included, with System controls. Time ratios on this shared VM have substantial
variation, including identical-IL controls. Source equivalence does not erase
observed timing residuals. Read remaining-costs.json and the full CSV tables.
Only managed allocation counters are measured; no total native/JIT/retained
memory claim is made. Compile-only EH timings can include eager JIT work, so
compile+first execution is reported separately.

Equal-operation follow-ups found no higher allocations in100 targeted
current-versus-published phase comparisons (52 modern,48 Framework). Small
fractional adaptive-count residuals did not reproduce at equal counts. Original
observations are preserved in full; no subtraction or replacement is performed.
Empty observer windows allocate0, so fractions are not called counter overhead.
See allocation-confirmation.json and allocation-confirmation-framework.json.

Individual Framework stages use exact isolated baselines described by their
manifests. The combined matrix is measured on the source commit in manifest.json.
Mutable Framework warm compilation was4376B pre-PR and4520B after the seven
initial round2 repairs (+144B), while nested mutation invocation improved24B->0B
and factory+one invocation208B->184B. Consult the final matrix for combined values.

Rejected experiments retain their evidence: terminal split/forced inlining;
unchecked scope indexing; Quote/Dynamic/RuntimeVariables outlining (held);
Volatile.Read pool guard; terminal Constant/Parameter helper split; generic Light
provenance checks; zero-argument typed signature arrays; wrapper ldloc/pop cleanup.
The latter hit a systematically higher .NET10 cold capture8 allocation mode in
the sample; its cause is unknown and cold parity was not claimed.
The binary-region extension is also held: balanced warm compilation improved
roughly12-15%, but Standard .NET10 cold scalar add remained5.75% slower in the
last9-pair trial (prior variants7.3-8.0%). Traces did not substantiate accidental
region-helper inlining as the cause. All8 correctness controls passed; neither
the extension nor its NoInlining gate is in the accepted source.

The deep mixed128 cold scope allocation excess of10328B is explained by seven
LabelScopeFrame growth arrays plus the compact pool table. Removing it generally
requires a capacity/layout tradeoff; no larger allocation in shallow cases is
hidden to improve that one shape. Small EH examples use inline scope buffers,
so their cold excess is not attributed to those overflow arrays.
